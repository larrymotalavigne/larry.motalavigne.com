## The conviction

A small-business website deserves the same rigor as a SaaS. No WordPress to
babysit, no opaque shared hosting: a fast, secure, monitored site whose every
update goes through a test pipeline.

## How I work

1. **Analysis of the existing site** — an automated audit (performance, SEO,
   mobile) grounds the proposal.
2. **Several design directions** — the client chooses from real, navigable
   mockups, not promises.
3. **Build and go-live** — the site is deployed on my infrastructure with
   TLS, monitoring and backups, just like my own products.

## Why an engineer rather than an agency

The same craftsman designs, builds and operates. No subcontracting, no
per-incident maintenance for a site that breaks: the infrastructure is
industrialized, so the site stays online, fast and current.
